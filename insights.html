SEC · Corp Fin

Expanded Nonpublic Review for Draft Registration Statements

Source: SEC Division of Corporation Finance

The staff's enhanced accommodations let any issuer, not only emerging growth companies, submit draft registration statements confidentially. Notably, issuers may now omit underwriter names from an initial submission, submit subsequent drafts regardless of how long they have been a reporting company, and use the process for de-SPAC transactions where the SPAC survives. Essential reading before setting an IPO timeline.

FASB · ASU 2026-01

Initial Measurement of Paid-in-Kind Dividends

Source: Financial Accounting Standards Board

ASU 2026-01 amends ASC 505 to require that PIK dividends on equity-classified preferred stock be measured using the rate stated in the preferred stock agreement, typically applied to the liquidation preference. The distinction between discretionary and nondiscretionary dividends no longer drives the measurement. Effective for annual periods beginning after December 15, 2026, with prospective or modified retrospective transition.
