Informational purpose only

The content on this website is general information about the services offered by State & Lake Advisory, LLC. It is not accounting, audit, tax, or legal advice, and it should not be relied on for any specific transaction, position, or filing.

No client relationship

Submitting the contact form or sending an email does not create a client relationship. A relationship begins only when both parties sign a written engagement letter that sets out scope, deliverables, timing, and fees.

Independence

State & Lake Advisory, LLC does not perform audits or attest services. Advisory work is scoped to avoid impairing the independence of a company's external auditor, and the firm does not act as auditor for advisory clients.

Illustrative scenarios

The situations described on this site are anonymized composites intended to show the type of work the practice handles. They do not describe specific named clients, and no confidential client information is disclosed.

Fees

Fees are confirmed in writing in the engagement letter before any work begins.
