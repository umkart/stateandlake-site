What We Do

Four areas of focused expertise

Each engagement is scoped to the specific issue at hand, whether it's a single technical position or ongoing fractional support across a single or multiple filing cycles.

Select any service area below to expand the full scope of work.

01Technical Accounting

Judgment-intensive GAAP and IFRS positions built to withstand audit and regulatory scrutiny.

  • Revenue recognition (ASC 606, gross vs. net)
  • Lease accounting (ASC 842 / IFRS 16)
  • Goodwill and long-lived asset impairment, CGU analysis
  • Business combinations and purchase accounting
  • Consolidations, including VIE, voting interest, and joint venture analysis
  • Stock-based compensation
  • Financial instruments and derivatives
  • Current Expected Credit Losses (CECL) standard adoption and ongoing application
  • Foreign currency translation and cross-border reporting
  • Discontinued operations and held-for-sale accounting
02SEC Reporting

Filing-ready disclosure support across the reporting cycle.

  • 10-K, 10-Q, and 8-K drafting and technical review
  • Non-GAAP measures and MD&A support
  • Disclosure checklists and filing-readiness review
  • SEC comment letter response support
  • Pro forma financial statements for IPOs, capital raises, debt issuances, and business combinations (Regulation S-X, Article 11)
  • S-1 registration statement preparation and support
  • IPO readiness assessments and roadmap planning
  • Auditor transition and RFP support, including firm selection, logistics, and continuity of financial reporting
  • ESG and climate disclosure readiness, including Disclosure Committee formation and SEC climate rule preparation
03SOX Compliance

Controls that hold up under audit and regulatory review.

  • Controls design and gap assessment
  • Remediation support
  • SOX 404 compliance readiness
  • Control deficiency severity assessment and aggregation analysis
  • SOX program buildout for newly public companies, including emerging growth companies (EGCs)
04Strategic & Fractional Support

Direct partnership beyond the technical memo.

  • Partnering with FP&A, IR, internal audit, or legal teams on strategic decisions and disclosure optics
  • Fractional or interim technical accounting director support
  • Board and audit committee-facing analysis and presentation support
  • Workshops and training seminars on technical accounting and reporting topics for finance teams
  • Equity compensation plan administration, including executive stock plan platform setup and 10b5-1 trading plan support

Contentious positions, tight filing deadlines, and audit disagreements need direct, senior-level support before they escalate.

Whether it's a one-time memo or ongoing fractional support during a busy filing period, let's talk before it becomes a bigger problem.
