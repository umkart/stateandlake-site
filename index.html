What We Do

Four areas of focused expertise

Each engagement is scoped to the specific issue at hand, whether it's a single technical position or ongoing fractional support across a single or multiple filing cycles.

01

Technical Accounting

Judgment-intensive GAAP and IFRS positions built to withstand audit and regulatory scrutiny.

  • Revenue recognition (ASC 606, gross vs. net)
  • Lease accounting (ASC 842 / IFRS 16)
  • Goodwill and long-lived asset impairment, CGU analysis
  • Business combinations and purchase accounting
  • Consolidations, including VIE analysis
  • Stock-based compensation
  • Financial instruments and derivatives
  • Current Expected Credit Losses (CECL) standard adoption and ongoing application
  • Foreign currency translation and cross-border reporting
  • Discontinued operations and held-for-sale accounting
02

SEC Reporting

Filing-ready disclosure support across the reporting cycle.

  • 10-K, 10-Q, and 8-K drafting and technical review
  • Non-GAAP measures and MD&A support
  • Disclosure checklists and filing-readiness review
  • SEC comment letter response support
  • Pro forma financial statements for IPOs, capital raises, debt issuances, and business combinations (Regulation S-X, Article 11)
  • S-1 registration statement preparation and support
  • IPO readiness assessments and roadmap planning
  • Auditor transition and RFP support, including firm selection, logistics, and continuity of financial reporting
  • ESG and climate disclosure readiness, including Disclosure Committee formation and SEC climate rule preparation
03

SOX Compliance

Controls that hold up under audit and regulatory review.

  • Controls design and gap assessment
  • Remediation support
  • SOX 404 compliance readiness
  • SOX program buildout for newly public companies, including emerging growth companies (EGCs)
04

Strategic & Fractional Support

Direct partnership beyond the technical memo.

  • Partnering with FP&A, IR, internal audit, or legal teams on strategic decisions and disclosure optics
  • Fractional or interim technical accounting director support
  • Board and audit committee-facing analysis and presentation support
  • Workshops and training seminars on technical accounting and reporting topics for finance teams
  • Equity compensation plan administration, including executive stock plan platform setup and 10b5-1 trading plan support
Case Studies

Case studies across key technical areas

These are composite, anonymized scenarios representative of the type of engagements State & Lake is designed to support.

Details below are illustrative composites reflecting common engagement types. No confidential client information is disclosed.

Business Combination: Purchase Price Allocation Under a Tight Close Timeline

Risk

Misstated fair values on acquired intangibles could distort post-acquisition earnings and draw auditor scrutiny

Approach

Built the PPA framework, valued acquired intangibles and contingent consideration, and coordinated with third-party valuation specialists to support the position

Outcome

PPA finalized within the measurement period with no post-close adjustments

Consolidations: VIE Determination for a Complex Ownership Structure

Risk

Ambiguous control indicators created uncertainty over consolidation conclusions ahead of quarterly reporting

Approach

Performed a full VIE analysis, evaluated power and economics criteria, and documented the consolidation conclusion for auditor review

Outcome

Consolidation position upheld with no restatement risk identified

Impairment: Goodwill Impairment Triggering Event

Risk

Unclear CGU boundaries following a market downturn, raising restatement exposure

Approach

Re-established cash-generating unit boundaries, built a defensible impairment testing methodology, and documented conclusions for board and audit review

Outcome

Impairment conclusion supported and accepted without further inquiry

Revenue Recognition: Position Challenged Pre-Filing

Risk

Potential restatement or delayed 10-Q filing

Approach

Built a documented ASC 606 position memo addressing principal-versus-agent conclusions, aligned with auditor expectations before filing

Outcome

Position accepted by auditors; filing proceeded on schedule

Leases: Portfolio Transition Under Time Pressure

Risk

Incomplete lease population and misclassification risk ahead of year-end close

Approach

Reviewed lease population for completeness, reworked classification methodology under IFRS 16, and documented judgment calls for audit support

Outcome

Clean disclosure with no audit adjustments at year-end

Stock-Based Compensation: Modification Accounting for an Executive Equity Award

Risk

Improper modification accounting could understate compensation expense and misstate diluted EPS

Approach

Assessed the award modification under ASC 718, quantified incremental fair value, and documented the expense recognition pattern

Outcome

Modification accounting accepted by auditors with no EPS restatement

Debt Issuances: Debt vs. Equity Classification on a New Note Issuance

Risk

Misclassifying embedded features could misstate liabilities and mislead covenant compliance reporting

Approach

Analyzed embedded derivative features, evaluated debt discount and issuance cost treatment, and documented the classification position

Outcome

Classification and amortization schedule accepted without audit adjustment

Supplier Financing Arrangements: Supply Chain Finance Disclosure Under New Requirements

Risk

Incomplete disclosure of supplier finance program terms could draw SEC comment letter attention

Approach

Mapped outstanding obligations under the program, built the required rollforward disclosure, and aligned presentation with ASU 2022-04

Outcome

Disclosure met new requirements with no follow-up SEC inquiry

Provisions and Contingencies: Loss Contingency Assessment Ahead of Year-End

Risk

Underestimating a probable and estimable loss could result in an understated liability and audit findings

Approach

Assessed likelihood and range of outcomes for pending matters, applied ASC 450 recognition criteria, and documented the accrual methodology

Outcome

Accrual accepted by auditors with adequate disclosure of remaining uncertainty
