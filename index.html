What We Do

Four areas of focused expertise

Each engagement is scoped to the specific issue at hand, whether it's a single technical position or ongoing fractional support across a filing cycle.

01

Technical Accounting

Judgment-intensive GAAP and IFRS positions built to withstand audit and regulatory scrutiny.

  • Revenue recognition (ASC 606, gross vs. net)
  • Lease accounting (ASC 842 / IFRS 16)
  • Goodwill and long-lived asset impairment, CGU analysis
  • Business combinations and purchase accounting
  • Consolidations, including VIE analysis
  • Stock-based compensation
  • Financial instruments and derivatives
  • Foreign currency translation and cross-border reporting
  • Discontinued operations and held-for-sale accounting
02

SEC Reporting

Filing-ready disclosure support across the reporting cycle.

  • 10-K, 10-Q, and 8-K drafting and technical review
  • Non-GAAP measures and MD&A support
  • Disclosure checklists and filing-readiness review
  • SEC comment letter response support
03

SOX Compliance

Controls that hold up under audit and regulatory review.

  • Controls design and gap assessment
  • Remediation support
  • SOX 404 compliance readiness
04

Strategic & Fractional Support

Direct partnership beyond the technical memo.

  • Partnering with FP&A, IR, internal audit, or legal teams on strategic decisions and disclosure optics
  • Fractional or interim technical accounting director support
  • Board and audit committee-facing analysis and presentation support

Revenue Recognition Position Challenged Pre-Filing

Risk

Potential restatement or delayed 10-Q filing

Approach

Built a documented ASC 606 position memo addressing principal-versus-agent conclusions, aligned with auditor expectations before filing

Outcome

Position accepted by auditors; filing proceeded on schedule

Lease Portfolio Transition Under Time Pressure

Risk

Incomplete lease population and misclassification risk ahead of year-end close

Approach

Reviewed lease population for completeness, reworked classification methodology under IFRS 16, and documented judgment calls for audit support

Outcome

Clean disclosure with no audit adjustments at year-end

Goodwill Impairment Triggering Event

Risk

Unclear CGU boundaries following a market downturn, raising restatement exposure

Approach

Re-established cash-generating unit boundaries, built a defensible impairment testing methodology, and documented conclusions for board and audit review

Outcome

Impairment conclusion supported and accepted without further inquiry
